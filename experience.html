Jun 2025 — Present Stanford, CA

Stanford Smart Sensing Systems Lab

Algorithm Development for Satellite Backscatter Communication

--

Implemented a signal processing algorithm for passive RF backscatter communication using existing SAR infrastructure. Partitioned the aperture into sublooks to encode bits, performed range/azimuth compression, and computed Signal-to-Clutter Ratio (SCR) per sublook and across sublooks. I also wrote a program to estimate the Radar Cross Section (RCS) of a point target from L0-RAW SAR data. The results for this were validated with theoretical estimates to within 2 dB.
I also simulated the effect of corner reflectors in raw SAR data by modifying the collected I/Q samples with a point target response. This allowed us to generate controlled ground-truth test cases for backscatter experiments without field deployment.

Sep 2025 — Present Stanford, CA

Navigation and Autonomous Vehicles Lab

Gaussian Splatting for 3D Scene Reconstruction and Analysis

--

I developed algorithms to benchmark 3D Gaussian Splatting optimizers to better understand how the software interprets geometry and color in a scene. This allowed me to understand the root causes of issues such as glare, which are a crucial weakness in most 3d reconstructions.
Another aspect of my work focused on building scene segmentation technqiues using density based clustering methods such as DBSCAN, HDBSCAN and OPTICS. The process improved 3D scene segmentation in unstructured environments and improve artificat detection in low-texture regions. This generalized approach towards scene segmentation was presented in the context of plant detection for burn piles at Stanford's Jasper Ridge Biological Preserve at the Stanford and Data Science Conference 2026.
