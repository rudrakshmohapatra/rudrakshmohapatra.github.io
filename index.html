About

I am a coterm student in the Electrical Engineering Dept. at Stanford University, where I'm studying the Software and Hardware Systems track. My academic interests lie in sensing systems and the algorithms that interpret them. I have worked on radar systems, 3D sensing and reconstruction methods in both my classes and research.

Outside of academics, I love playing badminton, painting, and taking walks across campus!

Highlighted Research

At Stanford, I am involved in research at the Stanford Smart Sensing (S4) Lab and the Navigation and Autonomous Vehicles (NAV) Lab. My work here has focused on utilizing Synthetic Aperture Radar for passive backscatter communication and 3D Gaussian Splatting for plant segmentation.
